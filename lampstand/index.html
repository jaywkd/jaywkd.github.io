Lampstand

A personal Android app for organising Bible-study materials.

Lampstand keeps study materials (PDFs, photos, videos, web links and practice sheets) in folders, topics and tags, and opens them next to a Bible reader with highlights, notes and verse links.

Device sync

If you choose to, Lampstand can keep a tablet and a phone in step through your own Google account. It stores its data in the hidden app folder of your Google Drive, which only Lampstand can open. It never reads your other Drive files.

Privacy policy

Lampstand is a personal project and is not sold or advertised.
