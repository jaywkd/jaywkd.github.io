Privacy policy

Lampstand for Android · last updated 13 November 2026

What Lampstand stores

Everything you add to Lampstand (materials, files, folders, topics, tags, Bible translations, highlights, notes and settings) is stored on your device. Lampstand has no servers of its own and does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or usage data.

Google account and Google Drive

Device sync is optional and off until you turn it on. When you connect a Google account, Lampstand asks only for the drive.appdata permission. This lets it read and write files in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Lampstand can open. Lampstand uses that folder solely to copy your Lampstand data between your own devices. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive, and it does not access your name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

Your data in that folder is not shared with the developer or anyone else. Google stores it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Limited use

Lampstand's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other network use

When you add a web link, Lampstand fetches that page to show its title and preview image. Nothing else about you is sent with that request.

Removing your data

Turn off device sync in Lampstand to stop syncing. To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Lampstand and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove Lampstand's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes the data on that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
